Chayanika Devi

Graduate Researcher - Health Data Science & Computer Vision

I am a Graduate Research Assistant at the Social Science Research Institute, University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, with 3+ years of research experience in the US and in international collaborations.

My health data research focuses on: 1) developing methods to monitor and evaluate health system performance, 2) using population and surveillance data (ACS, CDC, DHS) to measure healthcare access, telehealth coverage, and unmet health needs, and 3) building Python, machine learning, and GIS pipelines that turn complex health data into actionable evidence for more equitable care.

My computer vision research, presented at AANS and AAG, focuses on: 1) low-cost, open-source image-guided neuronavigation with 3D Slicer and SlicerIGT, 2) stereo and infrared camera calibration for 6DoF head pose tracking, and 3) AI-assisted medical image segmentation for CT and MRI. Below you can find my research and projects.

I am currently pursuing my Master's in Computer Science at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, and previously earned a Master's in Geographic Information Science at the University of Cincinnati, where I received the Women in GIS Graduate Scholarship.
