About

Chayanika
Devi

I'm a health data researcher based in Honolulu, Hawaiʻi, with 3+ years of research experience in the US and international collaborations. I use population and surveillance data (ACS, CDC, DHS) with Python, machine learning, and GIS to measure healthcare access, telehealth coverage, and unmet health needs in underserved communities. I also work on computer vision and medical imaging, from low-cost open-source neuronavigation with 3D Slicer to stereo and infrared camera calibration for 6DoF head pose tracking. I've published in BMJ Global Health and Frontiers in Digital Health, presented at AANS and AAG, and taught introductory Python to the next generation of computer scientists.
